# Web-Series-Analysis-1

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Indian web series data across various dimensions, including release trends, ratings, genres, platforms, and content volume. Here's a breakdown of the visual insights:

Trend of Web Series Released Per Year:

Displays a fluctuating trend from 2016 to 2024, with a peak in 2022 and a notable drop in 2023 and 2024.

Platform-Wise Average IMDB Rating by Genre:

Shows how genres perform across platforms like Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hotstar, and others.

Sci-Fi and Mystery genres tend to receive higher ratings across platforms.

IMDB Ratings Across Streaming Platforms:

Indicates most platforms maintain an average rating between 6 and 8, with Netflix appearing most frequently.

IMDB Rating vs. Number of Seasons:

Highlights that web series with around 2–3 seasons are most common and tend to maintain higher ratings.

Top 5 Highest Rated Web Series:

Lists "Leila," "Mumbai Diaries," "Ray," "She," and "Sunflower" as top-rated titles.

Genre in Ratings:

Sci-Fi and Mystery genres are associated with higher cumulative IMDB ratings.

Genre Distribution of Web Series:

Sci-Fi and Mystery are the most common genres, while Drama, Comedy, and Crime have relatively fewer titles.

Highlight Web Series:

Compares individual series by IMDB rating, showing consistent high ratings across popular titles like "Scam 1992," "Sacred Games," and "The Family Man."

Episodes and IMDB Rating:

One series with over 500 episodes stands out, likely a long-running title, though not necessarily correlating with a high IMDB rating.

Episodes Per Season Analysis:

Demonstrates variation in episode count across seasons for different series, with some series having consistent structure while others vary significantly.

Overall, the dashboard gives valuable insights into viewer preferences, genre popularity, platform performance, and content trends in the Indian web series space.
